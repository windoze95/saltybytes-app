SaltyBytes finds real recipes from across the web and gets out of your way. No ads, no pop-ups, no scrolling past somebody's life story to reach the ingredients.

FIND REAL RECIPES

Tell the agent what you're in the mood for — "cozy comfort food", "quick weeknight dinner", "something new with chicken" — and it searches the web for real recipes from real sites and creators. No AI-invented dishes.

• Results start streaming in seconds, and the agent keeps digging while you browse
• Roundup pages ("31 Best Casseroles") are opened up so the individual recipes inside come to you
• Top Picks: a curated shortlist of the best matches, with the agent's reasoning for every pick
• Tap any result for an instant preview — ingredients, steps, cook time — before you save it
• Prefer classic search? Flip the agent toggle off

IMPORT FROM ANYWHERE

• Paste any recipe link — including TikTok, Instagram, Facebook, YouTube, and Pinterest videos
• Snap photos of cookbook pages or import a PDF — pages with several recipes come in as separate recipes
• Dictate a family recipe out loud, paste raw text, or type it in manually
• Whatever the source, you get the same clean, structured recipe card

MAKE EVERY RECIPE YOURS

• Fork a recipe into a variant, or regenerate it with feedback — "make it dairy-free", "double the garlic"
• Follow every variant in a branching recipe tree
• Source measurements stay as written, with your units alongside: 2 cups (480 mL)

COOK FOR THE WHOLE FAMILY

• Build dietary profiles for each family member through a conversational AI interview
• Recipes and search results are checked against everyone's allergies and restrictions, with clear flags
• Hands-free cooking mode: say "next step" or ask "how much butter?" — no touching the screen with raw-chicken hands

WORKS WITH YOUR AI ASSISTANT

Add SaltyBytes as a connector in Claude or ChatGPT and search, preview, and save your recipes straight from a conversation.

PRIVACY

Sign-up needs only an email address. Your recipes stay yours: no ads, no data selling, no third-party analytics SDKs. AI processing happens only to fulfill your requests.
